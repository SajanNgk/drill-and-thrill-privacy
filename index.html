Drill & Thrill

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what Drill & Thrill (the "game", Android package com.drillandthrill.game) collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer (GitHub: SajanNgk).

1. The short version

  • The game works fully offline. Your progress is saved on your device.
  • We do not ask for your real name, address, phone number or payment details.
  • Signing in with Google is optional and is only needed to post scores on the online leaderboard.
  • The game shows rewarded ads (ads you choose to watch) and uses anonymous analytics and crash reports. In regions that require it, you are asked for consent first.
  • We do not sell your data.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (gold, drills, upgrades, trophies, settings such as sound) is stored locally on your device. We do not receive it unless you use the online leaderboard. Uninstalling the game removes it.

3. Online leaderboard and Google sign-in (optional)

If you sign in with Google, we use Firebase Authentication. We receive a unique account identifier from Google and use it to link your scores to you. The game does not use your Google email address or profile picture.

When you post a score we store, in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): your unique identifier, the display name you choose, your score, your depth, and the time of the run. Your display name and score are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name or any personal information as your display name.

4. Ads

The game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads (for example, to revive after a dig or to double gold). Ads are always optional. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and device and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under Google's own policies: policies.google.com/privacy.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game shows a consent message through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalised. You can change your choice any time from the privacy options button in the game's settings, where available. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android settings.

5. Analytics and crash reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is used and to fix crashes. These collect anonymous information such as gameplay events (for example, a dig started or finished), device model, operating system version, app version, approximate region, and crash diagnostics. Where consent is required, this collection is only turned on after you agree. We do not use this data to identify you personally.

6. Service providers

These providers process data on our behalf or under their own terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Functions): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Sign-In: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard is in use. To have your leaderboard entry and account identifier deleted, contact us using the details in section 12. Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase for its standard retention period.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us using the details below.

10. Security

Data is sent to Firebase over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected here.
